02 / STUDIO

Everything stays
on your machine.

Library, folders, favourites, profiles and slot bindings live in your roaming application-data directory — kept on update and on uninstall.

258 built-in presets

Cross 98, dot 59, circle 54, chevron 35 and T-shape 12, tagged by game. Filed in a sidecar, so a built-in is never modified.

Versioned transfer

Export writes your values as they are. Import lists every entry, conflict and repair first, and applies only what you approve.

Game profiles

Switch automatically when a game reaches the foreground — or restrict a profile so it stops switching rather than guessing.

Stretched compensation

4:3, 16:10, 5:4 and custom ratios keep the reticle square, plus ADS morphing and firing bloom with configurable decay.
